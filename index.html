Drone detection and tracking

98.3% accuracyComputer visionCNN
GitHub

Small drones pose a growing security challenge, as they are hard to detect accurately with a single sensor: camera performance drops in low light, and acoustic sensors are affected by ambient noise. To address this, we developed a multi-sensor AI system that combines three data sources: radio frequency signals, audio, and video. The system relies on convolutional neural networks (CNN), with a dedicated deep learning model for each source whose outputs are fused into a single decision, while the YOLOv8 computer vision model tracks the drone in real time.

I built the audio detection model and fused the outputs of the different models into a single decision.

Classifying Alzheimer's stages from MRI

98% accuracyCNN
GitHub

Early diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease helps slow its progression, yet reading thousands of MRI scans consumes much of doctors' time. We therefore developed a deep learning model that classifies brain MRI slices into four stages, trained on about 80,000 slices from 461 patients. Given how rare advanced cases were in the data, we addressed the class imbalance to ensure the model detects them accurately.

I built and compared several CNN models to reach the most accurate one.
